## CoReward-Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct

This is the Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct model trained by the Co-rewarding method using the MATH training set, as presented in the paper [Co-rewarding: Stable Self-supervised RL for Eliciting Reasoning in Large Language Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2508.00410).

For more details on the Co-rewarding framework and its implementation, please refer to our [Github Repository](https://github.com/tmlr-group/Co-rewarding).
